Downloadable derivative database: /roof-data/index.json lists all tiles.
Each /roof-data/{tile}.json.gz contains the gzip-compressed JSON geometry.
Coordinates are longitude/latitude in WGS84. Each record has an id and exterior
ring; ring closure is implicit. Holes and non-geometric properties are omitted.
The derivative database and extraction script remain available under ODbL 1.0.

National Geospatial Program. Imagery credits: USDA, USGS The National Map.
